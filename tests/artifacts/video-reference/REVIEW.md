## Target-line timing and camera limits

At **01:39.267**, full green lines connect selected infantry to the move destination. They remain visible through about **01:40.017**, disappear by **01:40.033**, and the health bars remain selected. This provides an approximately **0.77-second fully visible window** in this recording, not an engine timer constant or input-latency measurement. Their endpoints follow the units and converge on the destination; they are not permanent A* route polylines. Red attack lines around **01:33.9–01:34.3** disappear with selection clearing, so that interval does not independently measure their expiry.

During camera movement around **01:38.4–01:38.6** and **01:48–01:52**, terrain and units move under the fixed sidebar/command strip, with progressive shroud reveal. No pan velocity, easing curve, or scroll acceleration was calibrated. Dark fog-edge pixels and scrolling video artifacts should not be used as pixel-perfect terrain acceptance.

## Bounded acceptance checklist

- [ ] Fresh 90°/180° tank orders from rest: turn before meaningful sideways translation; report hull/travel angle and displacement, not facing alone. Then verify an already-moving bend without introducing a stop at every cell.
- [ ] Stationary attack changes turret bearing independently while hull and world anchor remain fixed. Preserve the verified after-build improvement.
- [ ] Straight travel crosses cell boundaries without periodic brake/surge; render interpolation follows simulation. A selected group through a narrow obstacle gap neither clips nor oscillates indefinitely; brief local waiting is acceptable.
- [ ] Standing/firing/walking/deployed poses reflect actual action and displacement. Capture an explicit stop and a blocked walker; those timings are not certified by this retail sample.
- [ ] At 800×600 / zoom 1, retain 168px sidebar and 60×48 cameos; match the six native command controls, segmented selection bars, original contextual cursors, and temporary unit-to-destination lines.
- [ ] Production spends credits progressively, advances its radial overlay and enters native Ready/tab-flash states; typography and counter formatting match native crops. Recheck labels/tooltips against implemented commands.

Uncalibrated: retail game-speed setting, absolute unit speed, exact ROT-to-wall-time conversion, cursor cycle timing, Stop-key latency, all collision cases, and retail options/startup typography. These are explicit limits rather than passed parity checks.
